Why colleges replace their existing setup with Campusr

Most campuses don't suffer from a lack of software — they suffer from too many separate tools that don't talk to each other.

How it usually works

  • ✕ An ERP built in 2007 that is painful to open on a phone.
  • ✕ Official notices posted on paper pinboards or emailed as attachments nobody reads.
  • ✕ Class notes scattered across five different WhatsApp groups and expiring links.
  • ✕ Students finding out the mess menu changed only after walking 15 minutes to the dining hall.
  • ✕ Hostel maintenance requests written in a physical register book that gets lost.

How Campusr does it

One mobile-first dashboard for students

Classes, room numbers, attendance percentages, and test schedules are pulled directly from your university system and shown on a fast, mobile-friendly screen.

A clean repository for study material

Students upload notes, formula sheets, and past papers organized neatly by branch, semester, and course code. Classmates upvote the most helpful ones.

Daily campus updates in real time

Mess staff update the daily menu in 30 seconds from their phone. Campus administration can push urgent weather or schedule circulars that ping everyone instantly.

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: January 2026

1. Who this is for

Campusr is provided by Campusr Technologies Inc. to licensed colleges, universities, and their enrolled students and faculty. Accounts are tied to validated institutional email addresses or registrar records.

2. Student data privacy

We do not sell student data, show external ads, or track students across the web. All class records, attendance logs, and personal details remain the property of the student and their institution in accordance with FERPA and standard privacy laws.

3. Acceptable use of shared notes

The notes exchange feature is strictly for student-authored summaries, study guides, and original revision material. Uploading pirated commercial textbooks, leaked exam papers, or copyrighted commercial material is prohibited and results in immediate account suspension.

4. Availability & support

We target 99.9% availability for all active campuses. Timetable views and mess menus are cached locally on device so students can still view their schedule if campus internet goes down.
